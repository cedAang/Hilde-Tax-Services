What we handle

Tax help for every situation

Individual & Business Tax Preparation

We prepare and e-file personal, family, self-employed, and business returns. We dig into your situation to find every deduction and credit you qualify for, and file securely as an IRS authorized e-file provider.

Tax Audits & IRS Assistance

Got an audit letter or IRS notice? Don’t panic. We explain what the IRS is asking for, organize your documents, and respond correctly. We stay with you until it’s settled.

Tax Resolution & IRS Issues

Owe back taxes or facing penalties, liens, or wage garnishment? We review your options, including payment plans and other relief, and work toward a resolution that gets you back in good standing with the IRS.

Amended Returns

Made an error on a past return, or missed a credit? We prepare and file an amended return to fix it, so you can recover refunds you left on the table.

ITIN & Identity Verification

We help you apply for or renew an Individual Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN) and clear IRS identity verification, so your return isn’t held up.

Prior-Year & Unfiled Returns

Behind on one or more years? We prepare your unfiled returns and get you compliant again as quickly as possible.

Bookkeeping

Keep your business finances organized all year, not just at tax time. We record income and expenses and reconcile your accounts. You always know where your business stands, and filing season goes smoothly.
